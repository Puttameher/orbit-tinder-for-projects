# Orbit product brief

## Register
product

## Product purpose
Orbit helps people discover projects, research, open-source work, and career opportunities with the clarity of a great recommendation, not the noise of an infinite feed. It learns explicit signals, exposes fit evidence, and turns mutual interest into reversible next actions.

## Users
Developers, AI/ML engineers, researchers, open-source contributors, students, and ambitious builders who want meaningful opportunities and collaborators.

## Tone
Grounded, capable, and human. The product should feel like an intelligent instrument, not a chatbot or an enterprise admin panel.

## Non-negotiables
- This first milestone is a design preview, not production implementation.
- Project fit is based on visible, declared context.
- A match requires mutual interest.
- Agent explanations show strengths, trade-offs, and missing information.
- Agent drafts never send messages or publish changes.
- A remix is a separate brief and never overwrites the source project.
- No production work begins until the owner explicitly approves a preview direction.
